A business analytics presentation exploring automotive sales performance through key performance indicators (KPIs), dashboard visuals, slicers, and concise business interpretations.
📌 Overview
🏁 Project focus: Turn automotive sales dashboard visuals into clear, evidence-aware business insights.
This project presents an automotive sales dashboard as a structured, presentation-ready analysis. It covers sales volume, revenue, profit, fuel-type distribution, vehicle-type performance, and city-level comparisons. Each analytical slide places a chart or dashboard visual alongside explanatory points to make the findings easier to communicate.
The presentation uses a professional automotive-inspired visual style, with a dark navy background, blue accents, and high-contrast typography.
🎯 Project Objectives
📈 Summarize headline performance indicators using KPI cards.
💰 Explore revenue and profit patterns across the available periods and cities.
🚙 Compare performance across vehicle and fuel categories.
🎛️ Demonstrate how a vehicle-model slicer can be used to filter dashboard views.
💡 Translate visual patterns into business observations and follow-up questions.
📣 Communicate analysis clearly through charts and presentation slides.
📊 Dashboard Components
📈 Revenue Trend Chart: Shows changes in revenue across the displayed years.
🏙️ Profit by City Chart: Compares profit performance across different cities.
🚙 Revenue by Vehicle Type Chart: Compares revenue generated by different vehicle categories.
⛽ Units Sold by Fuel Type Chart: Shows the distribution of vehicle sales across Petrol, Diesel, Electric, and CNG.
📍 Vehicle Distribution by City Chart: Compares vehicle counts across different cities.
📉 Revenue and Profit by City Chart: Compares revenue and profit across cities.
💡 Business Insights: Summarizes key patterns and observations to support data-driven decision-making.
🔍 Key Observations
Based on the supplied dashboard visuals:
Petrol is the largest category in the displayed units-sold-by-fuel-type chart.
SUV has the highest displayed revenue among the vehicle types in the chart.
The revenue trend declines across the years shown.
City-level charts show differences in the displayed revenue and profit measures.
The vehicle-model slicer supports model-specific comparisons across dashboard visuals.
These are observations from the visuals, not claims of causation. The underlying dataset and measure definitions should be reviewed before drawing business conclusions.

🚀 How to Use
Open the PowerPoint presentation in Microsoft PowerPoint or a compatible presentation application.
Review the dashboard visual on each analytical slide.
Read the corresponding explanation and key takeaway.
Validate displayed KPI values and reporting periods against the source dashboard or dataset before using them in business decisions.
⚠️ Data Quality and Limitations
Some displayed KPI formats may need validation, particularly Profit Margin and Average Satisfaction. Confirm their definitions, units, and percentage formatting against the source report. Also verify whether the final year shown in the revenue trend represents a complete reporting period. The presentation alone does not establish the cause of any trend.
No underlying dataset, data dictionary, refresh schedule, or measure definitions were supplied with the presentation; these should be documented if made available.
